CEO David Moezidis outlined Benchmark's strategic direction in a Phoenix Business Journal interview, emphasizing strong growth momentum driven by demand in semiconductor equipment, advanced computing, and AI infrastructure. The company is on a trajectory toward nearly 3 billion dollars in annual revenue, with its Path to 2030 vision focusing on expanding presence in high-growth markets across semiconductor, aerospace, defense, medical, and industrial sectors.
