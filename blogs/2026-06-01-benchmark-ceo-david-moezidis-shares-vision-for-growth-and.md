The Phoenix Business Journal recently profiled Benchmark CEO David Moezidis, highlighting the company’s strong growth momentum and strategic direction under his leadership. In the interview, Moezidis discussed how demand for semiconductor capital equipment, advanced computing, AI infrastructure and medical technologies is helping drive Benchmark toward annual revenue approaching $3 billion. He also shared how investments in customer relationships, manufacturing capabilities and a dedicated global business development strategy have contributed to the company’s recent success.
