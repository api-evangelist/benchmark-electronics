A recent EE Times Asia article highlights how Benchmark’s Thailand operations are helping customers succeed through high-mix, low-volume manufacturing, advanced engineering services, and a highly experienced workforce. From medical devices and aerospace systems to advanced communications and industrial technologies, the focus is on solving complex manufacturing challenges, not simply building products. The article also explores how Benchmark is investing in automation, engineering talent, and regional capabilities to support customers navigating evolving global supply chains.
